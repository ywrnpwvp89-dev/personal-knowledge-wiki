# Ingestion checklist

Capture title, type, author/publisher, dates, absolute path or URL plus page/heading/timestamp, scope, atomic facts with exact dates and units, actions/deadlines, and uncertainty. Recommended pages: `来源/`, `主题/`, `看板/近期截止日期.md`, `看板/待确认事项.md`, `index.md`, `知识库日志.md`, `.knowledge-manifest.json`. For scans mark OCR confidence; for blocked links record user-supplied text and mark page-specific details unverified.
